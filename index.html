FAQ

The questions every expat asks first.

Are you actually licensed?
Bali Fixer is a registered Indonesian PT PMA. Our legal work is run through our Bali-licensed law firm partners (SK Kemenkumham on file). We're not lawyers pretending to be a hotline — we're a hotline with real lawyers behind it.
How fast is "same day"?
For South Bali (Canggu, Seminyak, Kuta, Uluwatu, Sanur, Denpasar, Ubud) average dispatch is under 60 minutes, 24/7. North Bali, Nusa islands and East Bali typically 2–4 hours depending on location.
Do I have to be a member to call?
No. You can pay per-incident (pricing above). Members get priority dispatch, member rates on every fixer in our vendor network, a written visa & lease audit, and the hotline answers in under one minute.
What if I'm in trouble with police right now?
Call the hotline first. Don't sign anything, don't pay anything, don't leave. We have a translator on the line within 90 seconds and a Bali-licensed lawyer dispatched if the situation needs one.
Can you actually stop a deportation?
If you call us before the immigration office issues a final deportation order — yes, in most cases. Once a SKEP (deportation decree) is signed, we shift to fighting the entry ban and protecting your assets here. Speed matters: don't wait until day 60.
What's the difference vs. travel insurance?
Travel insurance pays you back later. We physically show up now. Most members keep both — and we'll fight your insurer for you to actually get the payout.
How do I cancel my membership?
One message in the app. No notice period, no exit fee. We'd rather you stay because we're useful, not because we trapped you.
