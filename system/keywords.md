# Keywords Lock

Ten locked areas (A0–A9). Each area = one keyword cluster. Every piece of
content belongs to exactly one area. If a piece doesn't fit any area, you're
either off-brand or you've found a new area worth locking.

**Format per area (11 rows):**
1. Primary — head term, must appear in title + H1 + first 100 words
2. Secondary — 3–5 supporting head terms
3. Long-tail intent — full phrases people actually type
4. Pain/fear — desperate, raw queries (the panicked Google search)
5. Trust signals — short phrases that build credibility for this area
6. Local modifiers — area names that stack onto any keyword
7. Forbidden — phrases that don't fit this area or brand
8. Meta title — 60-char ceiling
9. Meta description — 155-char ceiling
10. H1 pattern — fixed structure
11. Internal links — sister areas to link to from this one
