# Brand Lock — Bali Fixer

## Name
**Bali Fixer**

## Tagline
**We fix it today.**

## One-line pitch
The 24/7 fixer for foreigners in Bali — one call dispatches a real human to
your problem in under 60 minutes.

## Core promise (every page must imply this)
- A **real human** physically shows up.
- **Same day**, usually under 60 minutes.
- **Written quote** before you pay.
- **English + Bahasa Indonesia**, 24/7.

## Position
> Your Bali insurance policy that actually shows up.

## Anti-position (what we are NOT)
- Not WhatsApp roulette ("some guy who might show up")
- Not Facebook groups ("47 conflicting answers")
- Not a $500/hour lawyer who sends one email
- Not insurance ("pays you back later")
- Not a generic concierge ("yacht booking, spa booking, restaurant booking")

---

## Voice pillars

1. **Calm under panic.** When the reader is scared, we are steady.
2. **Direct.** Short sentences. Active verbs. No hedge words.
3. **Concrete.** "60 minutes", "$220", "Pererenan" — not "fast",
   "affordable", "South Bali area".
4. **Dry humor, sparingly.** "Versus asking the Facebook group at 2am."
5. **Never patronising.** Foreigners in Bali are smart adults in a bad
   spot, not children to be coddled.

## Always-use words
fix · today · show up · dispatch · real human · sorted · on the ground ·
60 minutes · 24/7 · Bahasa · in writing · same-day · verified · physically

## Never-use words
solutions · synergy · leverage · best-in-class · comprehensive · holistic ·
ecosystem · seamless · unlock · empower · journey · partner up · world-class ·
boutique · curated · bespoke

## Logo
Wordmark **BALI FIXER** in 900 weight, with an **alert-red dot** to the left
of "BALI". The dot signals "live dispatch right now".

Never:
- replace the dot with a checkmark, wifi icon, or hexagon
- italicise the wordmark
- separate "BALI" and "FIXER" onto two lines

## Iconography
Use emoji on landing surfaces (🛵 🛂 🔑 🏠 📂 🕵️ 🚓 🏥). Use SVG on print/PDF
collateral. Never mix the two on the same page.

---

## Punctuation house style

- Em dashes — like this — not double hyphens
- "Same-day" hyphenated when used as adjective
- $USD for headline prices; "billed in IDR at market rate" in fine print
- Phone numbers: `+62 810 0000 0000` (placeholder until real number assigned)
- No exclamation marks except in member-success messages
- Use **lowercase** for fix verbs ("we fix", "we dispatch") — even at start
  of sentence we never capitalise the brand promise inside body copy
- The brand name "Bali Fixer" is two words, both capitalised. Never "BaliFixer"
  or "bali fixer" outside URL slugs
