# Cadence

## Daily (5 minutes, morning)
- Open `/system/plans/current-sprint.md`.
- Mark anything you completed yesterday as ✅.
- Mark anything blocked as 🚧 with a one-line reason.
- Pick the single most important thing to ship today. Say it out loud.

## Weekly — Monday (20 minutes)
- Run `/plan-week`.
- Project-manager agent proposes the sprint.
- You approve, edit, or reject.
- `current-sprint.md` is overwritten.

## Weekly — Friday (15 minutes)
- Run `/audit` on whatever shipped this week.
- Reviewer returns punch lists. Address must-fixes Monday next.
- Update `/system/connect.md` section 6 site map.

## Monthly — first Monday of month (45 minutes)
- Re-read `/system/plans/seo-roadmap.md` end-to-end.
- Re-prioritise based on what worked / didn't work.
- Update keyword position log in `/system/research/keywords/`.
- Archive completed roadmap items.

## Quarterly — first Monday of Q (2 hours)
- Re-read every file in `/system/`.
- Update the lock where reality has diverged.
- Specifically: are the 5 personas still right? Are the 10 areas right?
  Is any banned word now necessary, or vice versa?
- Note the date and the change reason at the top of each updated file.

## When to break cadence
Almost never. The cadence is the lock. If you find yourself ignoring it
two weeks in a row, the cadence is wrong — change it deliberately during a
quarterly review, not in the moment.
