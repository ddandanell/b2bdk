# Pre-publish Checklist

Walk this list end-to-end before any content goes live. If a line fails, fix
it before publishing. Don't argue with the lock.

---

## 1. Area & persona
- [ ] Identified ONE area (A0–A9). Not two.
- [ ] Identified ONE persona (P1–P5). Not all five.

## 2. Keywords
- [ ] Primary keyword from the locked area is in the title.
- [ ] Primary keyword is in the H1.
- [ ] Primary keyword appears in the first 100 words.
- [ ] At least 2 secondary keywords appear in body.
- [ ] At least 1 long-tail intent phrase appears verbatim.
- [ ] At least 1 pain/fear phrase appears verbatim (sentence sounds like
      the persona's actual Google search).
- [ ] No forbidden words from `brand.md` OR the area's forbidden list.

## 3. Meta tags
- [ ] Meta title ≤60 characters, contains primary keyword.
- [ ] Meta description ≤155 characters: primary + one secondary + time
      signal + price signal.
- [ ] `og:title` and `og:description` set.
- [ ] Page `lang` attribute is `en` (or `id` for Bahasa pages).
- [ ] Canonical URL set.

## 4. CTAs
- [ ] Hero has the 3-CTA pattern (Call · WhatsApp · ghost tertiary).
- [ ] Phone CTA uses `tel:+62…`.
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA uses `wa.me/62…?text=` with the area-specific prefill.
- [ ] Sticky bottom dock present on mobile.
- [ ] Final CTA at end of page matches the persona's preferred channel from
      the cheat sheet in `personas.md`.

## 5. Voice
- [ ] H1 is a problem statement, not a feature statement.
- [ ] Sentences are short. No clause stacking.
- [ ] Concrete numbers, not vague adjectives ("60 min" not "fast").
- [ ] No banned words from `brand.md`.
- [ ] Dry humor used at most once.

## 6. Proof
- [ ] At least one quantitative trust signal in the trust line.
- [ ] Pricing shown (membership OR per-incident OR both).
- [ ] Pricing has the "Billed in IDR" footnote.
- [ ] At least one testimonial attached or linked.
- [ ] Footer has legal entity + address.

## 7. Internal links
- [ ] Links to `/membership` at least once.
- [ ] Links to 2 sibling fix pages (per `connect.md` matrix).
- [ ] No dead links.
- [ ] No external links to competitors.

## 8. Mobile
- [ ] Viewport meta tag present.
- [ ] Tapped through on a phone (or DevTools 360px) — no horizontal scroll.
- [ ] Inputs are ≥15px font-size (no iOS zoom).
- [ ] First CTA is above the fold on a 360×640 viewport.
- [ ] Page loads under 1.5s on 4G (no external fonts/heavy images).

## 9. Legal / safety
- [ ] No medical, legal, or immigration "advice" given as a Bali Fixer
      opinion. We coordinate professionals, we don't practice their craft.
- [ ] No language that endorses bribery, document fraud, or illegal entry.
- [ ] No promises of outcome ("guaranteed", "always", "100%"). Use "most",
      "usually", or quantified rates from real data.
- [ ] Hospital/medical content includes "if life-threatening, dial 118 first."

## 10. Final sanity
- [ ] If you read this page out loud to the persona, would they recognise
      themselves in the first 10 seconds?
- [ ] If a competing fixer service read this page, would they be jealous of
      one specific sentence?
- [ ] You ran through the 5-step loop in `README.md` without skipping.

Ship it.
