# Personas Lock

Five locked personas. Every piece of content is written for exactly **one**
of these. If your content tries to speak to all five, it speaks to none.

## When you write, ask in this order
1. Which persona am I writing for?
2. What did they type into Google?
3. What is the first sentence they need to read to feel "yes, this is me"?
4. What do they want to happen in the next 60 seconds?

If your draft doesn't pass all four, you haven't picked a persona — you've
written for "everyone", which is no one.
